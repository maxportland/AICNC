How the 7I96S side behaves

Step and direction are 5 V

  • Each STEP and DIR is a 5 V differential pair that can drive 24 mA. Wired as drawn, the + side goes to the driver's + input and the − side to its − input.
  • The other way, which Mesa suggests for drives that need more current: PUL+ and DIR+ to +5VP, PUL− to STEP−, DIR− to DIR−.
  • The CL57T and CL86T (v4) ship set for 24 V signals. Set their 5V/24V selector to 5 V.

The encoder, and TB3 left free

  • The spindle encoder takes 5 V from TB2-12 and returns to TB2-9. Jumpers W1–W3 choose differential (right) or single-ended TTL (left). With a single-ended encoder, wire only A+ and B+.
  • The config uses only the encoder's speed (encoder.00.velocity), so the index pair can stay empty.
  • Nothing is wired to TB3. The camera laser is switched by the 7I84U's OUT2, so all of the machine's I/O is on one card. OUT0–3 are floating DC solid-state relays (36 V, 2 A) and OUT4–5 are AC (100 mA), free for later.

Power and smart serial

  • P4 takes 5 V only: pin 1 (top, square pad) is +5 V and pin 2 is ground. Any other voltage damages the card.
  • The +5VP pins on TB1 and TB2 are 5 V outputs with resettable fuses (PTCs), for field wiring.
  • In this firmware, smart-serial port 1 is on P1, the 26-pin header, with channels 0–7. The 7I84 is channel 0 and the 7I83 is channel 1, both through an RS-422 breakout card on P1. TB2's serial pins (port 0) aren't used.

Check these before relying on it

  • IP address jumpers. The config talks to 10.10.10.10, the address stored in the card's EEPROM. That needs W4 down and W5 up. Both down (the factory default) gives 192.168.1.121.
  • Driver signal voltage. The closed-loop drivers' 5V/24V selector must be on 5 V, or they can miss steps or not see them at all.
  • P1 breakout power. W6 up sends 5 V out on DB25 pins 22–25 to power the breakout. Use it only with a Mesa breakout that expects it (for example a 7I44). The config doesn't record which breakout you have.
  • Encoder mode. W1–W3 must match the encoder: right for differential, left for single-ended.
  • Spindle analog. TB2-22 to 24 (the 7I96S's own spindle output) aren't used. The ±10 V speed command comes from the 7I83: analogout0 and analogena0 on its TB2.
  • Frame ground. Connect the top-left mounting hole, beside the Ethernet jack, to the machine's earth.
