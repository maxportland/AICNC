How the 7I84U side behaves

Inputs see voltage

  • An input is on when it's fed field voltage. A wire to ground or an open wire reads off. Each input has 22 kΩ to ground inside.
  • At 24 V it turns on above 14.4 V and off below 9.6 V.
  • Wire every switch from +24 V to the input. The home switches, probe, tool setter and E-stop are all normally closed, so a cut wire reads as triggered, which is the safe way to fail. That's why HAL uses the -not pins for the ones that trigger by opening.

Outputs supply +24 V

  • In sourcing mode the output terminal itself goes to +24 V when on. Wire the load's + to the terminal and its − to 0 V. Don't feed +24 V in from another source.
  • 500 mA per output, and 2 A in total for each group of eight (OUT0–7 share one driver chip). An output trips at about 1.5 A on a short.
  • No flyback diodes needed on valves or relay coils. The drivers have clamps built in.
  • OUT2 is the exception: it's set to sink (output_sink 0x0004), so when on it connects its pin to 0 V. That lets it switch the 5 V laser, whose + is fed from a small buck converter.

Power on TB1

  • VFIELDA (TB1-3, 4) powers TB3 and sets its input thresholds. VFIELDB (TB1-1, 2) does the same for TB2. Supply both or the card won't start.
  • Wire VFIELD straight to the supply. Never switch it through a relay contact or a switch.
  • VIN (TB1-5) can stay empty: jumper W1 in its left-hand (default) position takes it from VFIELDB.

Check these before relying on it

  • Field voltage. This page assumes 24 V DC. The valves, relays and the CL86Y's ENA must suit the same voltage. The CL86Y takes 5–24 V on ENA.
  • Drawbar driver alarm. If the machine faults as soon as it's turned on, the CL86Y's ALM is the other polarity. Change input-05 to input-05-not (Mesa7I96S.hal line 249).
  • Drawbar driver enable. OUT7 is inverted, so it's on (driver disabled) while the machine is off. If the A motor goes limp when the machine is on, set output-07-invert to false (line 256).
  • Drawbar valve. M64 P0 turns OUT5 on and should lower the motor (LOWER_IS_ON = 1). Before connecting the valve, meter TB3-22 to ground: about 24 V after M64 P0, 0 V after M65 P0.
  • The laser's 5 V. Set the buck converter to 5 V before connecting the laser (it runs on 2.6–6 V). Its ground must be this card's field ground. The 7I96S's +5VP won't work, because its ground is isolated from the 7I84U's field side. Never move the laser to a sourcing output: those give the full 24 V.
  • Current. Anything on OUT0 (spindle power) bigger than a small relay coil, such as a contactor drawing more than 500 mA, needs an interposing relay.
  • Smart serial cable. The 7I84U's J1 is an RJ-45 for a straight CAT5 cable. It goes to channel 0 of the RS-422 breakout card on the 7I96S's P1 header. In this firmware, smart-serial port 1 is on P1, and the 7I83 is on channel 1. It doesn't go to the 7I96S's TB2 serial pins, which are port 0 and unused.
