Milo 7I83 wiring

The 7I83 does one job on this machine: it sets the spindle's speed with a ±10 V signal and enables the spindle drive. Drawn from the HAL file and laid out like the real card, with the RJ-45 pointing left and TB2's pin 1 at the bottom. The spindle drive's terminals are taken from the SDF servo drive diagrams in reference_documents.

How the 7I83 side behaves

The analog output

  • Each AOUT gives −10 to +10 V, measured against the AGND pin beside it. Load it with 2 kΩ or more.
  • Mesa suggests shielded twisted pair: AOUT to the drive's command input, AGND to its reference, and the shield to AGND at the 7I83 end only.
  • The output is isolated from the 7I96S and the Pi, which helps avoid ground loops.

The enable output

  • Each ENA is a floating optocoupler switch: up to 100 V DC and 50 mA. It is polarised, so ENA+ must be the more positive side. Reversing it can damage it.
  • The enable also gates the analog output. While ENA0 is off, AOUT0 is held at 0 V.
  • If communication with the 7I96S stops for more than 50 ms, all outputs go to 0 V and every enable turns off. That doesn't replace an E-stop that removes drive power.

Power and serial

  • TB1-1 VIN takes 8–35 V DC (about 1 W). TB1-2 is ground, and it's connected to the analog ground.
  • Mesa suggests putting the VIN supply near the drive it signals to, for the best noise isolation.
  • J1 takes a straight CAT5 cable from channel 1 of the RS-422 breakout on the 7I96S's P1. That cable also powers the 7I83's serial side (30 mA).

Check these before relying on it

  • Speed polarity. With M3 the config sends a negative voltage (scale.0 gain −0.0034 V per rpm, so about −3.4 V at 1000 rpm). With M4 it sends a positive one (scale.1, +0.0034). The SDF analog-speed diagram shows a 0 to +10 V command. If the drive is set for 0–10 V only, M3 won't turn the spindle. Either set the drive for ±10 V, or change the gains and use its CW input for direction.
  • Drive ground. Mesa says to run a separate wire from the drive's signal ground to the 7I83's TB1 ground, besides the AGND in the twisted pair.
  • Enable polarity. The drive's SON input is sinking: COM+ (CN1-8) goes to +24 V, and SON (CN1-21) turns on when it's pulled to 0 V. So ENA0+ goes to SON and ENA0− to the drive's 0 V (DGND, CN1-16).
  • Which drive. The config doesn't name the spindle drive. The CN1 and CN6 pins shown come from the SDF series diagrams in reference_documents. Check them against your drive's label.
  • W1 jumper. Up (the default) is operate mode at 2.5 Mbaud, which the 7I96S needs. Down is setup mode at 115.2 kbaud.
